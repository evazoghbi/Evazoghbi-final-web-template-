Gold Coast

14th - 22nd of December 2023.

Exploring the Gold Coast with a few close friends, from its beaches and coastline to the city, shops and surrounding places. A collection of photographs and memories from time spent in Australia. Me and one of my closest friends went to the Gold Coast to visit our friend from school who moved away. This was so fun as she had lived there for a year at that point and knew some really cool spots. We explored and went to so many cool places and spots to swim which was really fun. We also did lots of shopping and eating out which was another highlight. It was great to see our friend as it had been a while since she moved and was an overall exciting trip. I've been to the Gold Coast many times throughout the years although this trip was such a highlight and a trip I'll never forget. It was one of the first trips I had been on without family and it was cool to explore and travel with just friends. Australia is also just such a nice place to enjoy summer, I love everything about the country, except maybe the spiders and would really love to move their one day as I feel its warm all year round unlike New Zealand and I have always said I need to live in a warmer climate, I feel it makes people more motivated and happier in general.
