About Me

ME !!

Heyy, my name is Eva Zoghbi, I am 19 years old and currently studying at Otago Polytechnic School of Design, completing my communication design degree. Design has been something I have loved for years, hence why I am now studying it. Its always been a way to express my creativity and enjoy life.

I love traveling and exploring new places!

Traveling has always been a major part of my life, with half my family living over seas. Ever since I was young we always used to travel, visit new places and create lasting memories as well as seeing family that I barely ever get to see. I've always loved the idea of seeing the world and experiencing different cultures. I mainly like warmer climates, so when traveling I am always convinced to go somewhere warm. I believe summer makes people happy!

I dream of traveling the world and experiencing all the amazing cultures and places it has to offer and want to create a collection of memories and experiences that I can share with others. After traveling the world I really want to settle down somehwere warm, with opportunities to continue exploring.

COLLECTED PLACES

Collected places is my personal memories and moments gathered through the travels of some special places.

I created this travel journal to bring the joy of my holidays to others and share my photographs, stories and details from beautiful places I have visited over the years. By exploring new cities and discovering cool cultures and beautiful summery places I have gathered a collection of my experiences spending time with family and friends with each place holding unique images and memories.

I created Collected Places as a way to reflect and share my memories, documenting them for others to see and admire and giving people a chance to see what beautiful things lie around the world. This is a place to remember and look back on all the amazing times I have had. I wanted to share not only where I have traveled but share the people, scenery, experiences and cultures as well sharing how each place was special to me.

Places I Collected ...

Beirut, Lebanon (June 23 - July 23, 2026) Four weeks traveling around Beirut, exploring the places, food, culture and family. In the mountains and on beaches.

Fethiye, Turkey (July 25 - August 1st, 2026) A week in Fethiye surrounded by beaches, mountains and the Mediterranean coast. Exploring the town, swimming in the sea, enjoying the warm weather and taking in the scenery.

Istanbul, Turkey (23rd July -25th July 2026) Exploring Istanbul for a few days, discovering the city’s history, architecture, food and busy streets. A place where old and new come together, with something interesting around every corner.

Fiji (16th -24th September 2023) A collection of memories from Fiji with my family, exploring the beaches, tropical landscapes and relaxed island atmosphere in a beautiful resort.

Gold Coast, Australia (14th - 22nd of December 2023) Exploring the Gold Coast with a few close friends and family, from its beaches and coastline to the city, shops and surrounding places. A collection of photographs and memories from time spent in Australia.
